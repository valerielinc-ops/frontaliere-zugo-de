Rollenüberblick

The Senior Medical Materials Coordinator provides medical, scientific and regulatory support for Switzerland and serves as the legally required person responsible for advertising in accordance with Art. 25 AWV (Arzneimittel-Werbeverordnung).

The role ensures that promotional and non-promotional materials are scientifically accurate, balanced and aligned with Swiss regulatory requirements.

Aligned with regional objectives and the Global Medical Plan, the Senior Medical Materials Coordinator supports local medical and promotional initiatives and scientific projects by applying medical and scientific expertise to the review and communication of clinical data and scientific evidence.
