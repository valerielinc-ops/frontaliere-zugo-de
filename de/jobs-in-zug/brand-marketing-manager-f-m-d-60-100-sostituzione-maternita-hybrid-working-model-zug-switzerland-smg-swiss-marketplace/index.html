Vollständige Beschreibung

Your Mission

This is a fixed-term position for 6–7 months, providing maternity leave cover. The anticipated

start date is 1 March 2027 , with flexibility to agree on an alternative date.

As a Brand Marketing Manager in the General Marketplace Business Unit, you will play a crucial

role in strengthening the Ricardo, tutti and anibis brands through impactful campaigns,

consistent brand development and engaging communication. This role allows you to manage

diverse marketing projects and collaborate with cross-functional teams while contributing to our

overarching goals and mission. Join our dynamic team, located in six countries (Switzerland,

Serbia, Vietnam, France, Germany, and India), where creativity thrives. Together, we tackle

challenges, celebrate successes, and keep learning and growing.

Specifically, you will work on

  • Managing integrated campaigns and marketing projects from planning to delivery, covering brand campaigns, product communications, tactical promotions and events, while coordinating internal and external stakeholders.
  • Coordinating the production of advertising materials and creative assets across digital and offline channels, ensuring brand consistency in collaboration with internal creative teams and external agencies.
  • Maintaining and evolving brand strategies and guidelines , including brand positioning, values, visual identity and tone of voice.
  • Collaborating with Content Marketing, Digital Marketing and CRM , as well as Product, Engineering and UX , to deliver clear and engaging communication about product features and updates.
  • Defining objectives and KPIs , monitoring performance and managing campaign and project budgets.

Qualifiche

What will you bring to the ride?

  • Strong project management skills and experience delivering integrated campaigns, with the ability to align and coordinate internal and external stakeholders.
  • A high level of ownership , combined with flexibility, team spirit and enjoyment of cross-functional collaboration .
  • Strong problem-solving skills and a practical approach, with the ability to build support for ideas and turn them into action.
  • A passion for creativity, technology and new ideas , including hands-on experience using AI tools for creative development, content creation and everyday workflows.
  • A keen eye for design and a strong feel for language , with strong communication skills in German and English. French is a plus.
  • Confidence using analytics tools , such as Google Analytics and Tableau, to turn data into actionable insights.
  • A qualification in marketing or communications and several years of relevant professional experience. An agency background or experience as a freelancer is a plus.

We value the skills you bring and your ability to learn quickly, so don’t worry if your career path

has been unique.
